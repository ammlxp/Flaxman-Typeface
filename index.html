flaxman

This is a work-in-progress revival of Flaxman,
a typeface designed by Edward Wright in 1967.
The typeface currently includes the original letterpress characters, with plans to expand it further through a new style that incorporates Spanish characters as a tribute to Wright's background. Several artists have previously digitised this typeface, and this project aims to continue that effort through open-source development, making it more widely accessible.
